**Current status (November 2026):** I do not currently have funding for new Ph.D. students, M.S. students, or postdocs. I am open to advising projects for undergraduate and M.S. students at the University of Oregon, and to other research collaborations.

Please read the FAQ below before emailing. It covers the questions I receive most often.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are you recruiting new students or postdocs?

Not right now. I do not currently have funding for new Ph.D. students, M.S. students, or postdocs.

### I'm already at the University of Oregon. Can I work with you?

Probably! I am open to advising research projects for undergraduate and M.S. students at the University of Oregon. Send me an email and attend a research group meeting. You will meet current students, learn about open projects, and see how you might contribute. Many students get started by helping with data preparation, feature design, or experimental evaluation. If you want to design new algorithms and publish in top venues, plan to take one of my courses (for example CIS 472/572: Machine Learning), do well, and budget substantial time—original research is demanding and iterative.

### Are you open to other collaborations?

Yes. If you have a research problem you think I could help with, send me an email describing it.

### How can I get a response to my email?

Send a personalized note that explains who you are, why you are interested in our work, and what you hope to accomplish. Copying text from my website or publications is not helpful. Mention the word **“moose”** in the subject line so I know you read this page. If you do not hear back within a few days, feel free to send a polite follow-up.
